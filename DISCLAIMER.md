# Medical and Privacy Disclaimer

Health Timeline is an information-recording and organization tool. It is not a medical device, diagnostic system, clinical decision-support system, or substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

The project may preserve AI-generated analysis when it appeared in a conversation, but such content is explicitly separated from source-attributed facts. AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading and should not be relied on for diagnosis, treatment, medication decisions, emergencies, or other clinical decisions.

If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency, seek appropriate emergency medical care rather than relying on this software.

Health information is sensitive. Users are responsible for deciding where their timeline and evidence files are stored, who can access them, and whether their use complies with applicable privacy, institutional, research, and legal requirements. Do not place identifiable health records in a public repository.
